Furin Fitness — Privacy Policy

Last updated: August 2026
DRAFT — this policy was drafted to give clients a real, honest baseline and is not a substitute for legal review. Recommend having an attorney review before relying on it for compliance purposes.

What We Collect

  • Contact information: name, email, phone number.
  • Onboarding/waiver information: date of birth, emergency contact, medical conditions or physical limitations you choose to disclose.
  • Payment information: processed directly by Stripe — we do not store your card details ourselves.
  • Fitness data: workout logs, nutrition logs, body metrics, and progress you enter into the Client Portal or Everfit.
  • Communications: messages sent through the portal's coach chat or support tickets.

How We Use It

  • To provide coaching, programming, and progress tracking.
  • To send you onboarding instructions, receipts, and program updates.
  • To personalize the AI coach chat using your logged stats and tasks.
  • To improve our programs based on aggregated, de-identified trends.

Who We Share It With

  • Stripe — for payment processing.
  • Everfit — for workout programming and check-ins.
  • Jotform — for collecting onboarding/waiver submissions.
  • Resend — for sending automated emails.
  • We do not sell your personal information to third parties.

Data Storage & Security

Your data is stored in a Supabase-hosted database with row-level security, meaning only you and your coach can access your individual records — other clients cannot see your data. Access to administrative functions is restricted to the business owner.

Your Rights

You can request a copy of your data, request corrections, or request deletion of your account and data at any time by contacting us below. Deleting your account does not retroactively undo waiver agreements already signed for services already rendered.

Medical Disclaimer

Health information you provide is used to inform your coaching program, but Furin Fitness is not a medical provider. Always consult a physician regarding medical conditions, and see the full Assumption of Risk & Liability Waiver you sign during onboarding.
