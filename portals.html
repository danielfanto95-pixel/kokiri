How this works:
• Non-trivial plans (anything risky, structural, or large) get logged here before I execute — Approval starts Pending. Flip it to Approved (editing the Plan column first if you want changes) to greenlight it; anything already Active/Done without your explicit request was small enough to just do.
• Status tracks real-world progress: Not Started → Active → Blocked (with a reason) → Done. Needs Scoping means I genuinely don't know what you want yet — tell me and I'll turn it into a real plan.
• Type is Feature / Content / Research / Personal / Recurring Operation / Life Objective (Life Objective rows mirror the Arc Objectives on Saga/Home — same table, shown here for visibility).
• Once a task hits Done, I log it — including source files/docs touched — to the local Kokiri Task Log folder (C:\Users\danie\Kokiri Task Log\, outside this repo, not deployed) and to the Change Log tab.
