📝 Global Instructions & Context

This applies everywhere intelligence is used across Kokiri — every agent's reasoning, the chat panel, the messaging bridge, and the autonomous runner. Divided into three sections so it stays organized as it grows; all three are combined into one context block behind the scenes.

Section A

General Context

Standing facts and preferences that apply everywhere — tone, timezone, priorities, things to never do without asking first.
Section B

Current Saga

The active "quest" from your Sagas sheet, plus its stats and goals — surfaced to every agent so they know the big-picture arc you're in right now.
Section C

Agent Context & Instructions

Standing instructions for how agents should behave, beyond any single agent's own Purpose field — split into what you've explicitly told them vs. patterns Kokiri has picked up over time.
Scope: this is global context — not per-agent. Individual agents still have their own "Purpose" field (set on the Agent Dashboard) for role-specific instructions; everything above gets combined and prepended alongside that for every call, everywhere.
